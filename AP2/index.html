Red Dead Redemption 2

Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018) is an open-world action-adventure game developed by Rockstar Games. Set in the late 19th century, the game follows the story of Arthur Morgan, an outlaw and member of the Van der Linde gang, led by the charismatic Dutch van der Linde.

Featuring stunning graphics, a vast and highly detailed world, the game blends intense shootouts, exploration, richly narrated side quests, and an honor system that influences gameplay. The story explores themes of loyalty, survival, and the decline of the Wild West, as Arthur grapples with his choices and the conflict between a life of crime and redemption.

Gameplay includes hunting, interactions with NPCs, camp customization, and a dynamic system of random events. RDR2 is praised for its deep storytelling, memorable characters, and immersive world, making it one of the greatest gaming experiences ever created.

Cyberpunk 2077

Cyberpunk 2077 (2020) is an open-world RPG set in the dystopian metropolis of Night City, where power, technology, and body modifications rule. You play as V, a mercenary chasing immortality after a heist gone wrong. With the digital ghost of legendary rockerboy Johnny Silverhand (played by Keanu Reeves) stuck in your head, you must navigate a web of gangs, corporations, and cyber-enhanced criminals to survive.

The game blends deep storytelling with FPS combat, hacking, and player-driven choices that shape the world and multiple endings. Despite a rocky launch, Cyberpunk 2077 offers a visually stunning world, gripping side quests, and a mature narrative about identity, power, and human cost in a hyper-capitalist future.

With its immersive atmosphere, strong voice acting, and the Phantom Liberty expansion, it has evolved into a must-play for sci-fi RPG fans.

Grand Theft Auto V

Grand Theft Auto V (GTA 5) (2013) is an open-world action-adventure game developed by Rockstar Games. Set in the sprawling, satirical city of Los Santos (a fictionalized Los Angeles), the game follows three very different criminals: Michael, a retired bank robber living in witness protection; Franklin, a young street hustler looking for bigger opportunities; and Trevor, a violent, unpredictable meth dealer with a short fuse.

The story weaves between their chaotic lives as they pull off high-stakes heists while evading law enforcement, rival gangs, and their own personal demons. GTA V combines explosive action, driving, shooting, and mission-based gameplay with a massive, interactive world full of side activities, from golf and stock trading to illegal street races and bizarre strangers.

Praised for its sharp satire, engaging storytelling, and unmatched freedom, GTA V remains one of the best-selling and most influential games of all time, with its online counterpart, GTA Online, continuing to expand years after release.

Detroit Become Human

Detroit: Become Human (2018) is a narrative-driven adventure game developed by Quantic Dream. Set in a futuristic Detroit where lifelike androids serve humans, the game explores themes of artificial intelligence, discrimination, and free will through the interconnected stories of three androids: Connor, a detective android tasked with hunting deviants; Kara, a caretaker android who develops a motherly bond with a young girl; and Markus, a revolutionary leader fighting for android rights.

The game is a branching cinematic experience where every choice and action dramatically impacts the story, leading to multiple possible endings. With its stunning visuals, emotional performances, and heavy emphasis on player agency, Detroit: Become Human delivers a gripping, thought-provoking experience that questions what it means to be alive.

Praised for its ambitious storytelling and moral dilemmas, the game stands as one of the most immersive interactive dramas in gaming.

Watch Dogs 2

Watch Dogs 2 (2016) is an open-world action-adventure game developed by Ubisoft. Set in a vibrant, tech-savvy rendition of San Francisco, the game follows Marcus Holloway, a brilliant young hacker who joins the activist group DedSec to take down the corrupt corporations exploiting people's data through the city's interconnected surveillance system, ctOS 2.0.

Unlike its more serious predecessor, Watch Dogs 2 embraces a playful, satirical tone, blending hacking, stealth, and third-person shooting with a focus on non-lethal takedowns and creative problem-solving. Marcus can remotely manipulate gadgets, drones, and vehicles, turning the city itself into a weapon. The game encourages freedom in approaching missions—whether through stealth, hacking, or chaotic disruption.

With its colorful open world, engaging characters, and sharp critique of modern tech culture, Watch Dogs 2 is praised for its fun gameplay, improved mechanics, and lighter, more relatable story, making it a standout in the hacker-sandbox genre.

Battlefield 5

Battlefield V (2018) is a first-person shooter developed by DICE and published by Electronic Arts, set in World War II. The game emphasizes large-scale warfare with its signature "All-Out War" multiplayer battles, featuring up to 64 players in modes like Conquest and Breakthrough, where teamwork, vehicle combat (tanks, planes, and transports), and dynamic destruction play key roles.

The single-player War Stories campaign offers short, cinematic missions based on lesser-known events of WWII, highlighting different perspectives of the conflict. Multiplayer includes the Firestorm battle royale mode (now less active) and Grand Operations, a multi-day, objective-driven experience.

Praised for its immersive gunplay, stunning visuals, and realistic sound design, Battlefield V also faced criticism for its live-service model and initial lack of content. However, post-launch updates added iconic battles, weapons, and factions, solidifying it as a solid entry for fans of tactical, large-scale shooters.

Farcry 5

Far Cry 5 (2018) is an open-world first-person shooter developed by Ubisoft. Set in the fictional rural county of Hope County, Montana, the game pits players against Joseph Seed, a fanatical cult leader, and his zealous followers known as The Project at Eden’s Gate. As a rookie sheriff's deputy, you must build a resistance, liberate outposts, and dismantle the cult’s grip on the region through guerrilla warfare.

The game emphasizes chaotic freedom, allowing players to approach combat with guns, explosives, or recruited allies ("Guns for Hire"), including a memorable cast like the sniper Grace Armstrong or the bear Cheeseburger. With its vast open world, dynamic events, and cooperative multiplayer, Far Cry 5 delivers explosive action, dark humor, and a divisive but bold narrative tackling themes of extremism and blind faith.

Praised for its gameplay and Montana setting but critiqued for its story pacing, it remains a standout in the Far Cry series for its sandbox mayhem and eerie atmosphere.

Farcry New Dawn

Far Cry New Dawn (2019) is a standalone spin-off and sequel to Far Cry 5, set 17 years after a global nuclear catastrophe. The game takes place in a vibrant, post-apocalyptic version of Hope County, Montana, now transformed by the "Superbloom"—a colorful but dangerous ecosystem where nature has reclaimed the ruins of civilization.

Players take on the role of a survivor helping to rebuild the community of Prosperity, while battling the Highwaymen, a ruthless gang of scavengers led by the twin sisters Mickey and Lou. The game retains Far Cry's signature open-world chaos, with outpost raids, crafting, and "Guns for Hire" companions, but introduces Expeditions—missions to scavenge resources in far-off locations like a flooded amusement park or a crashed aircraft carrier.

With its lighter RPG elements (tiered weapons/gear), vibrant art style, and ties to Far Cry 5's story, New Dawn offers a shorter, more focused experience. It's praised for its fun gameplay loop and creative take on post-apocalyptic settings, though some critics found it less ambitious than mainline entries. A solid pick for fans of chaotic, loot-driven shooters.

Just Cause 3

Just Cause 3 (2015) is an open-world action-adventure game developed by Avalanche Studios. Set on the fictional Mediterranean island of Medici, ruled by the ruthless dictator General Di Ravello, you play as Rico Rodriguez—a chaos-loving, grapple-hook-wielding mercenary on a mission to liberate the island and ignite a revolution.

The game is all about over-the-top destruction and sandbox freedom. With Rico's iconic wingsuit, parachute, and retractable grapple hook (now upgraded for tethering objects), you can unleash creative havoc—flinging enemies into explosives, hijacking vehicles mid-air, or toppling military bases with reckless style. The map is packed with towns, military installations, and scenic landscapes to explode, liberate, or just joyride through.

While criticized for performance issues and repetitive objectives, Just Cause 3 excels as a pure physics-driven playground, where causing mayhem is the main goal. Its explosive gameplay, sunny setting, and lack of seriousness make it a blast for fans of chaotic, unscripted fun.

Máfia: Definitive Edition

Mafia: Definitive Edition (2020) is a full remake of the classic 2002 game, developed by Hangar 13. Set in the 1930s in the fictional city of Lost Heaven, the game follows Tommy Angelo, a cab driver turned mobster, as he rises through the ranks of the Salieri crime family. The story is a gripping tale of loyalty, betrayal, and the consequences of a life of crime, told with cinematic depth and emotional weight.

The game combines narrative-driven missions with open-world elements, though it focuses more on its linear, story-heavy design rather than sandbox chaos. The remake features stunning visuals, revamped gameplay mechanics, and enhanced voice acting, bringing the Prohibition-era world to life with period-accurate cars, music, and atmosphere.

Praised for its storytelling, character development, and immersive setting, Mafia: Definitive Edition is a love letter to the original, offering both nostalgia and a fresh experience. While its driving and combat feel dated to some, it stands as one of the best crime dramas in gaming.

Máfia: Definitive Edition

Mafia II: Definitive Edition (2020) is a remastered version of the 2010 cult classic, developed by Hangar 13. Set across the 1940s-1950s in the fictional cities of Empire Bay and Lost Heaven, the game follows Vito Scaletta, a war veteran turned mobster, as he climbs the ranks of the Mafia in a world of betrayal, violence, and shifting allegiances.

This edition bundles the base game and all DLCs (Joe's Adventures, Jimmy's Vendetta), with upgraded visuals, 4K resolution, improved lighting, and refined character models. The story remains a highlight—a gritty, cinematic tale of crime, family, and the American Dream gone wrong, backed by a stellar period-accurate soundtrack (featuring artists like Dean Martin and Muddy Waters).

Gameplay blends third-person shooting, driving, and open-world exploration, though the world is more atmospheric than interactive compared to modern sandboxes. The combat and driving feel weighty, emphasizing realism (e.g., speeding attracts police, gas is limited).

Battlefront 2

Star Wars Battlefront II (2017) is a multiplayer-focused action shooter developed by DICE, in collaboration with Criterion and Motive Studios. Set in the Star Wars universe, the game spans all three eras of the saga—Prequel, Original, and Sequel Trilogies—letting players battle as iconic heroes (like Darth Vader, Luke Skywalker, or Rey) or as troopers on the ground in large-scale warfare.

The game features:

- Galactic Assault & Supremacy: Epic 20v20 objective-based battles with vehicles and heroes.

- Starfighter Assault: Space dogfights with ships like X-wings and TIE fighters.

- Co-op & Single-Player: Includes a story campaign (following Iden Versio, an Imperial special forces soldier) and offline modes.

Initially controversial for its loot box system (later removed), the game underwent major updates, adding free maps (e.g., Geonosis, Scarif), heroes (e.g., Obi-Wan, General Grievous), and a class-based progression system.

Praised for its authentic Star Wars atmosphere, visuals, and sound design, Battlefront II became a redemption story, now regarded as a must-play for fans of the franchise.
